How to use the course

Three modes of study.

01

Guided course

Follow Chapters 1–24 in order. The sequence introduces perception before engineering, then rendering, experiments, computation, and advanced applications.

02

Reference mode

Use search, filters, and the glossary to enter directly at a specific concept such as psychophysics, force control, texture rendering, machine learning, or mid-air haptics.

03

Research pathways

Follow a curated route for device design, perception, computational haptics, wearables, contactless haptics, XR, or medical applications.
